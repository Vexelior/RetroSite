Welcome!
to The World Wide Wayback — a homepage about the homepages of the 1990s

Thanks for stopping by! Grab a Surge, pull up your chair, and get comfy, because this page is all about how the World Wide Web looked back when you had to get off the phone to go online.

In the 1990s, anybody with a text editor and a free GeoCities account could build a homepage. There were no style sheets to speak of (not until late 1996, and hardly any browser supported them properly for years), no web fonts, and no layout tools except <TABLE>. People made do with what they had, and what they had was blinking text, scrolling marquees, tiled backgrounds, animated GIFs, and an unshakeable belief that every page needed a hit counter.

Everything on this site is built the way it would have been in 1997: HTML 3.2 tags, <FONT> tags, nested tables, BGCOLOR attributes, frames, and GIFs. View the source and see for yourself! (In Netscape, that's View > Document Source.)
